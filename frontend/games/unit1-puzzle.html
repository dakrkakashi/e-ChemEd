Industrial Problem

Scale Formation

Occurs in high-pressure thermal zones; leads to boiler wall overheating and localized tube rupture.

Caustic Embrittlement

Dangerous intercrystalline cracking in boiler rivets, bends, and highly stressed joint seams.

Sludge Formation

Soft, slimy non-adherent precipitate accumulating in colder parts of the boiler vessel.

Underlying Chemical Cause

Loose, slimy precipitate formed by MgCl2 or MgCO3

Salts with higher solubility in hot water that settle as loose slimy mud in sluggish flow zones.

High alkalinity (NaOH) attacking boiler metal stress points

Infiltrates microscopic hair cracks via capillary action, dissolving iron to form sodium ferroate.

Hard coating formed by CaSO4 precipitation on hot surfaces

Forms a hard, tenacious insulating crust directly on heat transfer surfaces (retrograde solubility).
